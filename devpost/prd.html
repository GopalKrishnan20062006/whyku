Understand every move.

A board-first Sudoku tutor. The engine establishes a deduction; the interface makes its evidence visible; AI explains only the verified record.

The product in one minute

  1. Open a curated puzzle directly on the playable board.
  2. Enter digits; candidates update from Sudoku rules.
  3. Request a hint when stuck and inspect a verified single.
  4. Reveal only as much help as needed, then make the move.

Trust boundary

Deterministic engine: chooses and validates a naked or hidden single and records supporting candidates or positions.

AI: explains that record. If the explanation fails its check or times out, a deterministic template is shown. No record means no AI explanation.

The board renders evidence from the record; it does not guess which cells to highlight.

Layout and input

  • Warm off-white notebook surface, ink-like digits, subtle grid lines, restrained accents.
  • Given digits are locked and visually distinct from editable entries. Number keys enter into the selected editable cell; focus does not jump after entry.
  • Candidate marks are visible by default; mouse and keyboard digits 1–9 are supported.
  • Selecting a cell marks its row, column, and box. Rule-based candidates update immediately after entry or erase.
  • Solution mismatch warnings are separate from candidate legality and enabled by default; they never reveal the correct digit.

Illustrative board layout only; the shown puzzle is not a claim about the curated puzzle set.

Hint journey

1 · NudgeGentle clue. No cell or digit.
→
2 · AreaRelevant row, column, or box only.
→
3 · TechniqueName technique; show relevant evidence; mask digit.
→
4 · ReasoningFull evidence and why it proves the move; digit hidden.
→
5 · RevealShow target and digit; offer explicit entry.

At every level: reveal next, return to the puzzle, or dismiss. Evidence stays highlighted while the hint is open, including when returning to the puzzle. Dismissal clears it; entering the move clears old evidence and requests a fresh deduction.

Evidence shown on the board

Naked singleAt Technique: target cell outlined; unit softly shaded; one remaining candidate shown as masked and eliminated candidates marked unavailable. At Reasoning: existing digits that rule out alternatives are visible; survivor stays masked.
Hidden singleAt Technique: relevant unit and all cells where the digit is a legal candidate are marked; target position is distinct but digit stays masked. At Reasoning: masked candidate appears in each eligible cell; only one position remains, explained in text.

Use consistent evidence colors: soft blue for relevant unit, amber for target, muted/crossed marks for eliminated candidates, and distinct outlines for positions being compared.

Important states

No supported single“No naked or hidden single is available right now.” Explain singles-only limit; offer entry review or another puzzle. No AI explanation without a verified record.
Solution mismatchFlag a locally legal but solution-wrong entry without showing the answer. Keep updating candidates, but block hints and provider calls. Say: “Correct or erase the flagged entry before requesting a hint.”
Rule contradictionIdentify conflicting cells; explain correction is needed before a reliable hint. No explanation request from a contradictory state and no solution reveal.
SolvedOnly a full grid matching the solution counts. Show “Puzzle solved. You worked it out!” and offer another puzzle.
Full but incorrectNever show success. Highlight conflicting entries and let the player edit or erase them.

Review the behavior by level

Levels 1–2 · Nudge and Area
No exact cell or digit is identified. The nudge may refer to an area/constraint; Area highlights only the relevant row, column, or box.
Level 3 · Technique
Name naked single or hidden single and relevant unit. Show type-specific candidate evidence, identify the target cell visually, but conceal the target digit.
Level 4 · Reasoning
Give complete reasoning based on the recorded evidence. AI prose is shown only when it passes the record check; on failure or timeout, show a deterministic template for the same record.
Level 5 · Reveal
Show target digit and offer an explicit action to enter it. On entry, clear highlights and recalculate from the updated board.
